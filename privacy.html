This Privacy Policy explains how Twilight Tree handles information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require accounts and we do not directly collect personal information. Game progress (high scores, upgrades, recently played) is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. It never leaves your device.

2. Advertising & Cookies

This Site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

3. Log Data

Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical info (IP, browser type, OS, referrer, timestamps) for security and analytics. This is not linked to any personal identity.

4. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

5. Children's Privacy

Twilight Tree is a general-audience site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EEA or California, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we do not directly collect such data, most requests are handled through Google's ad settings linked above.

7. Consent

By using this Site, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will appear on this page with an updated date.
