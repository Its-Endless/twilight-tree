How to Play

There is no wrong way to play. Here are the basics.

Movement

W A S D — walk
Shift — run
Space — jump / swim up
Ctrl — crouch
F — toggle flight (Creative)

Interaction

Mouse — look
Left click — break block
Right click — place block
Middle click — pick block
1–9 / wheel — hotbar

Menus

E — inventory & crafting
Esc — pause
T — toggle time speed

First steps

1. Walk toward the trees.
2. Break some wood.
3. Open your inventory (E).
4. Craft a workbench and a tool.
5. Build somewhere you like.
