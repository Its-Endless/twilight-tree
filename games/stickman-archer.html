HOW TO PLAY

🏹 DRAG BACKWARDS anywhere on screen — like pulling a real bowstring. The arrow fires in the opposite direction of your drag.

👆 Release to loose the arrow. The further you drag, the stronger the pull (and the further the string draws back).

🎯 Arrows fall with gravity — only the start of the flight path is shown. Lead your shots!

💀 Hit the head for 3× damage and an instant kill on normal enemies.

❤️ Dodge incoming arrows — your health is on the top-left.

🪙 Each kill gives score and coins. Spend coins on upgrades.

🔥 The longer you survive, the tougher and faster enemies become.

⌨️ Space shoot · P pause · M mute
